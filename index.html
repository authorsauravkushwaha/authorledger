03 — HOW I LIKE TO WORK

A few things
I believe.

Not a manifesto. Just useful principles I try to bring to the next thing I make.

01

Make it useful.

Start with the real problem. The best feature is often the one that makes a tool simpler and more dependable.

02

Stay honest.

Be clear about what software can do, what it cannot, and where a human should remain in the loop.

03

Keep learning.

Build in the open, take feedback seriously, and treat each project as a chance to understand something better.
