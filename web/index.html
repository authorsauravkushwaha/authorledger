      Two promises, kept in the code

      These aren't marketing lines — they're design rules this codebase enforces on itself.

      01

      The ledger is append-only, on purpose.

      A compliance record you can quietly rewrite isn't a compliance record. Audit entries are written once and never edited or deleted — not by you, not by the app, not by anyone. Corrections get their own new entry, timestamped. That's the whole point.

      02

      No detector theatre. Ever.

      The Writing Pattern Report never outputs a confidence score, a percentage, or a verdict about whether text “is AI.” No lightweight statistics tool can honestly claim that, and one that pretends to can talk an author into a wrong answer with false confidence. Only you know how a chapter was actually written.

      How it works

      Four steps from blank page to defensible disclosure record.

      1. Create a book for any novel, non-fiction project, or short collection you are writing.
      2. Write directly on the website with instant autosave, distraction-free focus mode, chapter outlines, and version checkpoints.
      3. Stamp each chapter honestly — Human-written, AI-assisted, or AI-generated — recorded in an append-only audit trail.
      4. Download anytime: export your compiled manuscript (.md, .txt, print-ready .html), KDP compliance report, or full JSON backup.

      Details worth knowing

      The questions authors actually ask, answered plainly.

      Where is my writing actually stored?

      Inside your browser — IndexedDB with a synchronous localStorage mirror — on your own device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and no account exists to leak. The optional local Python server instead keeps everything in a plain SQLite .db file you can back up like any other file.

      What does “append-only” mean for the audit trail?

      Entries are written once and never modified or deleted. Reclassifying a chapter adds a new entry rather than changing the old one; deleting a chapter or an entire book leaves the record that it existed. That's what makes it an honest paper trail instead of a mood board.

      Is AuthorLedger an AI detector?

      No — and it deliberately refuses to pretend to be one. The Writing Pattern Report shows plain statistics (sentence rhythm, word variety, repeated phrases) to help you remember how a chapter was written. It never outputs a probability or a verdict. Only you can classify your work honestly.

      Do I need to disclose AI-assisted editing?

      Amazon KDP's policy requires disclosing AI-generated text, images, or translations; ordinary AI-assisted work like brainstorming or grammar-checking your own writing does not currently require disclosure. Policies change, and Amazon reviews back-catalogues too — always check KDP's current guidelines. AuthorLedger keeps the record either way, so you can answer confidently.

      Can I take my work with me?

      Always. Export your compiled book as Markdown, plain text, or print-ready HTML; download individual chapters as .md; and export or restore complete JSON backups of the whole workspace. There is no lock-in and no proprietary format — your words are yours.

      Does it work offline?

      Yes. Once the page has loaded, writing, autosave, pattern reports, craft checks, compliance reports, snapshots, and exports all run with the network switched off. Only the optional Claude feedback needs a connection — and your own API key.
